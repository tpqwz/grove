share/wordnet-syn.txt is a list of WordNet 3.1 synonyms: the closest
senses only, eight words at most. WordNet is Copyright 2006 by Princeton
University. The list is used by the Concepts search. The classical word
families are Grove's own and are not part of WordNet.
